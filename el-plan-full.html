Section 5: Progression & Recovery

Sleep7–9 hours nightly. Non-negotiable for recomposition.
DeloadEvery 6–8 weeks, reduce volume by 40–50% for one week.
Progressive overload+2.5–5% weight when all prescribed reps completed with good form.
Cardio60–70% max HR to preserve muscle while in a deficit.
FibreHandful of spinach or kale in every roll; one piece of fruit daily.
UPF managementUPFs retained. Wholefood add-ons are non-negotiable for micronutrients.
Cost managementOwn-brand UPFs. Batch-cook. Soak dried legumes vs canned.
RecoveryIf excessively fatigued, +100–200 kcal from complex carbs on training days.
